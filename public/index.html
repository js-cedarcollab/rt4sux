How this works

Observed means the bus appeared in Metro's real-time feed. We record each scheduled trip's predicted arrival at the stop; the last prediction seen before the bus left the feed is our stand-in for the actual arrival time. It is a proxy, not an exact GPS timestamp.

Thresholds: early if more than 1 minute ahead of schedule; late if more than 5 minutes behind. A trip is final 30 minutes after its scheduled time. Placeholder thresholds, not Metro's official definition.

"No bus observed" means a bus may not have run or Metro's tracking feed had a gap. Real-time data near the start of a route has a history of gaps, and these stops are near the start. It does not prove a trip was canceled.

"Not monitored" means our own polling had a hole around that trip. Those are never counted against Metro.

Schedule: only weekday commute windows are watched (about 7:30-9:30 AM and 4-6 PM). Trips outside those windows and weekends are not tracked.
